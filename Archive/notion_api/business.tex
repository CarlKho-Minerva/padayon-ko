\section{Executive Summary}

Padayon Ko is an AI-powered platform designed to empower Filipino students to access higher education through scholarships. We address the critical need for a centralized, user-friendly resource that simplifies the complex scholarship application process. Our platform offers personalized scholarship matching, achievement tracking, essay writing assistance, and test preparation tools, all powered by cutting-edge AI.  We target both high schools and universities (B2B) as our primary customers, with a supplemental revenue stream from engaged parents (B2C).  Our vision is to expand throughout Southeast Asia and globally, becoming the leading platform for connecting students with educational opportunities and ultimately expanding into job hunting resources.


\section{Problem}

3.3 million Filipino students annually forgo college due to financial constraints.  The existing scholarship landscape is fragmented, making it difficult for students to discover and apply for opportunities.  Padayon Ko solves this problem by providing a centralized, AI-powered platform that streamlines the entire process.

\section{Solution}

Padayon Ko offers a comprehensive suite of tools:

* **Personalized Scholarship Matching:** AI-powered matching based on student profiles.
* **Achievement Tracker:** Transforms accomplishments into compelling narratives.
* **Essay Writing Assistance:**  AI-powered tools for generating and refining essays.
* **Test Preparation:** Practice bots for Math (Mathieu) and English communication (Fluent).
* **Application Tracker:** Keeps students organized and on track with deadlines.
* **Multilingual Support:** Available in English, Tagalog, and Cebuano.

\section{Market Analysis (TAM, SAM, SOM)}

We use a top-down approach, starting with the total student population:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{TAM (Total Addressable Market):}  23.85 million students in the Philippines.  We filter this down by our target demographic (Junior & Senior High) resulting in 14.31 million students.
    \item \textbf{SAM (Serviceable Available Market):}  Assuming 50\% urbanization, our SAM is 7.155 million students.
    \item \textbf{SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):}  Targeting a conservative 3\% school penetration rate in the first year, our SOM is 214,650 students, representing approximately 28 high schools and 3 universities.
\end{itemize}
